# MVIS Blender Reference Image Prompt Design

## Purpose

Create a consistent, technically credible reference image for a later Blender visualization of the Indian Railways Machine Vision Inspection System. The image is intended for visual approval and art direction; engineering dimensions continue to come from the supplied top-view and cross-section drawings.

## Intended Audience

Indian Railways officials reviewing the proposed inspection-site arrangement and the visual story of a train passing through the MVIS capture zone.

## Selected Visual Direction

Use an engineering-photorealistic architectural visualization rather than science-fiction concept art. Show the complete installation in an elevated three-quarter daytime view, composed like a professional railway infrastructure proposal. The result should be realistic, orderly, easy to understand, and suitable for a presentation slide.

## Required Site Structure

- Four straight, parallel Indian broad-gauge railway tracks on realistic ballast and concrete sleepers.
- A WAP-7 electric locomotive with Indian Railways LHB coaches passing through the active MVIS line.
- Realistic 25 kV AC overhead electrification: steel masts, cantilevers, insulators, messenger wires, droppers, and contact wires aligned over all tracks.
- An open trackside MVIS array with no gate, arch, gantry, tunnel, platform, or obstruction above the train.
- CAM-1 and CAM-2 as adjustable upper area-scan cameras on opposite sides at approximately 1.8 m height.
- CAM-3 and CAM-4 as lower area-scan cameras on opposite sides at approximately 0.6 m height.
- CAM-5 as a protected under-track line-scan enclosure centred between the active rails, with its glass lens visibly facing upward.
- Eight 150 W trackside inspection lamps, four on each side, aimed across the train body and underframe.
- Six narrow under-track LED strips around CAM-5, aligned across the inspection zone.
- Entry and exit axle sensors plus a clearly separate relay/control cabinet beside the active line.
- A fenced maintenance boundary and a safe service path.
- A dark green project-office portacabin beyond the fence, completely clear of every track and aligned parallel to the rails.

## Materials and Environment

Use weathered steel rails, concrete sleepers, brown-grey ballast, galvanized equipment poles, matte industrial camera housings, protective concrete pit surrounds, restrained safety-yellow lighting hardware, Indian Railways red-and-cream rolling stock, and natural daylight. The site should look maintained but operational rather than pristine or futuristic.

## Composition

- Landscape 16:9 frame at presentation resolution.
- Elevated three-quarter camera, high enough to show all four tracks and office clearance but low enough for cameras and the CAM-5 lens to remain legible.
- The MVIS inspection zone is the visual centre.
- The train occupies the active line without hiding all trackside equipment.
- The portacabin remains visible in the middle distance beyond the fence.
- Mild depth of field only; infrastructure must stay readable.

## Exclusions

Do not generate an overhead portal, gateway, scanning arch, station platform, passenger station, roof canopy, tunnel, trees blocking the installation, futuristic holograms, floating UI, neon cyberpunk lighting, extra railway tracks, cabin on rails, cameras attached to the train, distorted wheels, incorrect track gauge, disconnected overhead wires, logos invented for Indian Railways, written labels, dimensions, or watermarks.

## Prompt Deliverables

Produce two generator-specific prompts with the same visual contract:

1. A structured Nano Banana prompt that strongly emphasizes reference-image adherence and spatial relationships.
2. A descriptive GPT Image prompt with explicit positive details and a compact negative-instruction section.

Both prompts must explain that the generated image is a visual reference for a Blender build, not a substitute for CAD verification.
